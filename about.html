GET TO KNOW US

More than a directory.
A simpler way to find help.

VahanSathi is a web-based roadside assistance discovery platform developed with a simple idea: make it easier for people to find automobile service providers in their local area.

Whether a bike stops working, a car develops a mechanical problem, an auto-rickshaw needs assistance or a vehicle requires towing, users can explore relevant service listings through one convenient platform.

The platform provides service categories, local provider information, direct calling options, location support and map directions. It is designed to reduce the effort involved in finding roadside assistance.
