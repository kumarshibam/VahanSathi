# 🚗 VahanSathi – On-Road Assistance

**VahanSathi** is a web-based roadside assistance platform designed to help vehicle owners find nearby mechanics, puncture repair shops, towing services and other emergency vehicle support across **Sambalpur District, Odisha**.

The platform aims to make roadside assistance easier, faster and more accessible without requiring users to create an account or log in.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://kumarshibam.github.io/VahanSathi/

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Find Nearby Help** – Discover roadside assistance providers in Sambalpur.
- **Multiple Services** – Find mechanics, tyre repair, towing, key assistance and more.
- **Location Detection** – Use GPS to identify your current location.
- **Emergency SOS** – Quick access to emergency assistance options.
- **Provider Directory** – Browse service providers with contact information.
- **Direct Calling** – Contact service providers directly.
- **Responsive Design** – Accessible on mobile phones, tablets and desktops.
- **No Login Required** – Use the platform without registration.

## 📍 Service Coverage

Currently focused on:

**Sambalpur District, Odisha, India**

The platform is designed with a district-focused approach to help users discover roadside assistance services within the selected region.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of VahanSathi is to reduce the difficulty of finding roadside assistance during unexpected vehicle breakdowns.

It provides a simple digital platform where users can discover relevant service providers, access their contact details and request assistance directly.
